## What this means for the back-fill (PLAN §10)

Rough estimates from the figures above:

- **Storage:** about 1.46 GB found in total. 1.06 GB of it is 28 debate files over 5 MB, mostly plan sheets, which are recorded but not mirrored (PLAN §5). Mirroring everything else takes about 0.4 GB, well inside R2's 10 GB free tier. Postgres stores metadata only.
- **OCR:** debate and minutes PDFs nearly all have a text layer (2% and 0% of sampled pages need OCR). The scanned material is committee decisions (about 29%) and the hub's plan documents (about 89%, largely drawings, where OCR adds little). OCR is not the bottleneck.
- **LLM extraction:** only urban-planning debates need field extraction: 21 of the 125 debates are PUZ/PUD, plus the hub's 14 projects. Sending only the relevant pages of their memoriu, RLU and specialist report means a few hundred pages, roughly 0.5M tokens. That fits the free tier or a few dollars, not the $10/month budget.
- **History depth:**
  - **Debates** go back to 2020, which covers the 2020-and-later launch window.
  - **Minutes** go back to 2007, past the 2011 PUG target.
  - **Agendas** keep no history: only the current one is online.
  - **Notices** list only recent articles, but sequential IDs allow walking older ones.
  - **CTATU** files are only linked for the current year.
- **Request budget at 2.5 s each:** debates and hub are about 1,100 documents, about 45 minutes. Minutes are about 365 PDFs, about 15 minutes. Walking about 8,000 notice IDs back to 2020 would take about 6 hours, so it needs night runs split across several nights (PLAN §10.5).
