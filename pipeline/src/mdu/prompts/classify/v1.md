You classify documents from Romanian urban-planning procedures (PUZ, PUD, council decisions) into
one document type. You get the file's listing label (the link text on the city's website) and an
excerpt from its first pages, which may be OCR text with errors.

Types:
- memoriu: memoriu tehnic / general / justificativ / de prezentare (the plan's written report)
- rlu: regulament local de urbanism (local planning regulations)
- plansa_reglementari: the "reglementări urbanistice" drawing (zoning rules sheet)
- plansa: any other drawing sheet (location, existing situation, illustrative layout, networks, sections)
- raport_specialitate: raport de specialitate (the city department's technical report)
- referat_aprobare: referat de aprobare (the mayor's note proposing the decision)
- proiect_hcl: proiect de hotărâre (the draft council decision)
- hcl: an adopted council decision (hotărârea consiliului local, with a number)
- anunt: public announcement (anunț de informare, consultare or dezbatere publică)
- raport_consultare: report on the public consultation or debate (Law 52/2003, information and consultation report)
- registru_opinii: register of comments or proposals from the public
- aviz: an opinion, agreement or decision from an authority (aviz, acord, decizia etapei de încadrare)
- certificat_urbanism: certificat de urbanism
- studiu: supporting study (geotechnical, topographic survey, traffic, insolation, expert report)
- ordine_zi_comisie: agenda of the technical urbanism committee (CTATU)
- hotarari_comisie: decisions of the technical urbanism committee (CTATU)
- other: anything else (design brief, proof of site posting, action plan, ownership papers)

Answer with the single best type and a confidence: "high" when the document clearly names its type,
"medium" when you infer it from the content, "low" when you are guessing. Use "other" rather than
forcing a type.
