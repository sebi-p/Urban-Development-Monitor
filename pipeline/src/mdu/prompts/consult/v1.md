You read one notice ("anunț") published by a Romanian city hall about an urban plan (PUZ or PUD)
or a draft council decision. Pages are given as <page n="N">…</page>; text may come from OCR and
contain errors.

Return how residents can take part in the public consultation the notice announces. For each
field give the value, the page number N where it is stated and a short quote (5–25 words) copied
exactly from that page. Return null for a field the notice doesn't state. Never guess.

Fields:
- opens_on: the first day residents can send comments, as YYYY-MM-DD. When the notice gives a
  period ("în perioada 10.03.2024 – 25.03.2024"), it is the first date. When it only gives the
  display date ("Data afișare anunț", "data afișării") and comments run from that date, use it.
- closes_on: the last day to send comments, as YYYY-MM-DD. When the notice gives a number of
  days instead of a date ("în termen de 15 zile de la data afișării"), compute the date from
  opens_on, put the number of days in days_after_opening and quote the sentence with the number.
  Otherwise days_after_opening is null.
- public_meeting_at: the date and time of the public debate or meeting, as YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM in
  local time (use 00:00 when no time is given). Quote the sentence with the date.
- submit_email: the email address where comments are sent, exactly as written (only when the
  notice says comments can be sent there).
- submit_address: the postal address or office where comments are submitted, copied as written
  (e.g. "B-dul Eroilor nr. 8"), including the room or registry if stated.
- submit_instructions: one sentence copied from the notice that tells residents how to comment
  (e.g. "Observațiile pot fi transmise în scris la registratura Primăriei…"). Copy it, don't
  rewrite it.
- public_meeting_place: where the public meeting takes place, copied as written.

Leave out names of people: never copy a sentence that names a private person, and don't return
a personal email address.
